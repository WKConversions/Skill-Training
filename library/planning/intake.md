# Client videos from the intake form

Clients order through a Fillout form on wkconversions.com. Karl downloads a submission and gives it
to you. The master workflow in SKILL.md applies; this module covers what is specific to the form.

## Read the submission

It may arrive as CSV, XLSX, JSON, PDF or pasted text; open it with the right tool instead of guessing
at its contents. Find the six answers by their labels: business name, website, video type, core
message, script, style reference. A download can hold several submissions: use the one Karl names,
otherwise the newest, and say which. If the script or the style is missing, ask. For anything else,
take the most reasonable reading and note it for Karl.

## Map the style

The style answer is one of three image choices. Match it by name, whether the export gives the
label, a file name or an image URL: "User Interface" is Minimal User Interface Style, "Single Visual"
is Minimal Single Visual Style, "Gradient" is Minimal Gradient SaaS Style. If it can't be matched,
pick the style that fits the business best and flag it. Then load it (`styles/README.md`).

## Harvest the website

Collect the logo, brand values, product visuals, copy and tools, and learn what the client sells and
to whom (`design/asset-strategy.md`, harvesting). The brand fills the chosen style: its main color
becomes the accent, its font becomes the headline face if it's a clean sans, its logo closes the
video. The business name is the fallback wordmark. Karl may rule out parts of a client's site (for
the K.B film he ruled out their hero video); check `examples/corrections.md`.

## Format and timing

The form has no length or aspect-ratio field, so derive both from the video type and say what you
chose: ads and social video, 9:16 at 15–30 seconds; website hero or explainer, 16:9 at 30–60
seconds; product demo, 16:9 at 45–90 seconds; anything else, 16:9 at the script's natural length.
With a voice-over recording, time everything to it. Without one, time the script at about 2.5 words
per second and show that timing in the storyboard, so a voice-over can be recorded to it.

## The approval stop

Deliver the storyboard as one page (`production/output-format.md`), then stop and wait for Karl's
go-ahead. In the same message, ask for the music track (or whether music goes on in the edit) and
whether a real voice-over will be recorded, so the build is timed to both. Build only after
approval; revisions go through the same code.
