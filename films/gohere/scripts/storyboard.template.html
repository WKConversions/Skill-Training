Notes

  • Every key action lands on its spoken word: the website on “websites”, the photos swiping on “photos”, the route drawing on “directions”, the tile moving in the portal on “portal”.
  • Real proof, as the owner asked: the four client apps (Terschelling Tips, Transavia Tips, Ciao Tutti, BarcelonaTips) with their own app icons and colours, and the real portal layout and tile types.
  • Sample content follows GoHere's own demo: Rome, and “Your favourite restaurant” as the placeholder tip, so no real business is given invented details.
  • Music is not in yet. The brief asks for calm, optimistic music low in the mix; it goes under the voice-over at about −20 dB.
  • For the website hero, use the muted file for autoplay; the version with voice suits LinkedIn, sales follow-ups and fairs.
