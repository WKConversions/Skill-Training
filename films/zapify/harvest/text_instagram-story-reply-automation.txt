How to Make Story Automation Part of Your Workflow
Turn every Story reaction into a conversion — no manual replies needed.
1
Connect Instagram and create a Story trigger
Link your Instagram Business account and set up a Story automation. Choose whether to trigger on any Story reply, specific emoji reactions, or keyword-based responses. You can create different automations for different Stories — so a product Story sends a shop link while a Q&A Story sends a booking page.
2
Write your automated DM response
Craft a natural, on-brand DM that delivers value immediately. Include your link, offer, or call-to-action. Zapify supports AI-powered personalization so the message adapts to the follower's name and the context of their reply. The goal is a response that feels like you typed it yourself — just faster.
3
Post Stories and let Zapify handle the rest
Upload your Story as usual. Every reaction and reply triggers your automation instantly. Track how many DMs were sent, links clicked, and emails collected per Story in your Zapify dashboard. Use the data to learn which Story formats and CTAs drive the most conversions, then double down on what works.
