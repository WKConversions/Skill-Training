How to Make Email Collection Part of Your Workflow

Turn every Instagram interaction into a subscriber — automatically.

1

Create your lead magnet and email gate

Prepare the resource you'll deliver (PDF, video, discount code, course access) and upload the link to Zapify. Then configure the email gate — this tells Zapify to ask for an email before delivering the resource. You can customize the ask message to match your brand voice.

2

Set triggers across posts, Stories, and DMs

Choose how followers opt in: comment keywords ("EBOOK", "FREE", "GUIDE"), Story replies, or DM keywords. When a follower triggers the automation, Zapify asks for their email in a friendly, conversational DM. Once they reply with a valid email, the resource is delivered instantly.

3

Export emails and nurture your list

Every collected email appears in your Zapify dashboard, tagged by the trigger that captured it. Export as CSV and import into ConvertKit, Mailchimp, MailerLite, or any email platform. Now you have a segmented list of warm leads who already engaged with your content — the highest-quality subscribers you can get.
