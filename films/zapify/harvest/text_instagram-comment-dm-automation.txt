How to Make Comment Automation Part of Your Workflow
From setup to your first automated DM in under 5 minutes.
1
Connect your Instagram Business Account
Link your account through Meta's official OAuth flow. No passwords shared, no third-party access tokens. Zapify is a verified Meta Business Partner, so your account stays safe and fully compliant with Instagram's terms of service.
2
Choose your posts and set trigger keywords
Select specific posts, enable "Any Post" mode, or set up "Next Post" to auto-activate on your next upload. Then define the keywords that trigger a DM — common ones include LINK, INFO, PRICE, BUY, and GUIDE. You can use multiple keywords per automation and customize the response for each.
3
Write your DM response and go live
Craft a natural, conversational DM that includes your link, offer, or call-to-action. Zapify supports AI-powered personalization so each message feels human. Hit activate — and every matching comment from this point forward triggers an instant DM. Track performance in your Zapify dashboard in real time.
