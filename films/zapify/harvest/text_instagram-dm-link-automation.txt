How to Make Link Delivery Part of Your Workflow
One setup powers unlimited link deliveries — across all your posts.
1
Connect Instagram and add your links
Link your Instagram Business account through Meta's official flow. Then add the URLs you want to deliver — product pages, affiliate links, course enrollment, lead magnets, or anything else. You can add as many links as you need and map each one to a different trigger.
2
Set keywords and automation triggers
Define what triggers each link delivery: comment keywords (LINK, BUY, GUIDE), Story replies, or DM keywords. Zapify matches incoming messages against your triggers and fires the right response instantly. You can run multiple automations simultaneously across different posts.
3
Activate and track performance
Go live and watch your links get delivered automatically. Every DM sent, link clicked, and email collected is tracked in your Zapify dashboard. Use the data to optimize your captions, refine your keywords, and double down on what converts. Your followers get instant gratification, and you get a hands-free revenue engine.
