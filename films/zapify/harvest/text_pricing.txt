FAQ
Common questions about Zapify plans and billing
🟨
How much does Zapify cost?
Zapify offers a 30-day Free Trial ($0, no credit card), Starter at $9.99/month, and Professional at $19.99/month. Both paid plans include unlimited automations, and yearly billing gives you 2 months free.
🎁
Is Zapify free to try?
Yes. Start with a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. You get 3 active automations, 1,000 AI comment replies, and 1 connected Instagram account to test Zapify before upgrading.
🚀
Which Zapify plan should I choose?
Starter is ideal for creators and solo businesses who want more automation and reply volume. Professional is designed for growing brands and teams that need more scale, multiple accounts, and priority support.
📊
Does Zapify charge based on followers or contacts?
No. Zapify uses simple flat-rate pricing and never charges based on follower count, audience size, or contacts.
⚡
How does Zapify compare to other Instagram automation tools?
Many automation platforms increase pricing as usage or audience grows. Zapify keeps pricing simple with predictable monthly plans built specifically for Instagram automation.
📅
Do yearly plans save money?
Yes. Annual billing gives you the equivalent of 2 months free compared to paying monthly.
🔄
Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime?
Yes. Change or cancel your plan anytime from your dashboard. Your account remains active until the end of your billing period, and your settings stay saved.
🎧
Is customer support included in every plan?
Yes. All plans include support, with faster response times and priority assistance available on higher tiers.
🔍
Are there any hidden fees or setup charges?
No. There are no setup fees, onboarding charges, or hidden costs. You only pay for the plan you choose.
🛡️
Is Zapify safe for my Instagram account?
Yes. Zapify runs on Meta's official Instagram APIs and does not require password sharing or risky automation methods.
