FAQ
Got questions?
We've got answers.
Clear answers before you connect your Instagram account or launch your first automation.
⚡
What can Zapify do for me?
Zapify automatically replies to Instagram comments and DMs to capture leads, answer questions, and turn engagement into conversations, even while you're offline.
🛡️
Is Zapify safe? Will my account get banned?
Yes. Zapify is a Meta approved Tech Provider, runs on Meta's official Instagram API, and uses secure authorization. No bots or unofficial workarounds.
🔒
Do I have to share my Instagram password?
Never. Connect through Instagram's official secure login (OAuth). Zapify receives permission to automate, not access to your password.
📱
Does Zapify work with my Instagram account?
Zapify works with Instagram Business and Creator accounts. If you're using a personal account, switching is free and only takes a minute.
⏱️
How long does setup take?
Most users connect Instagram and launch their first automation in just a few minutes, no technical setup required.
✈️
Can I automate Instagram DMs and comment replies?
Yes. Trigger DMs and replies automatically from comments, keywords, story replies, and follower actions to respond faster and automate repetitive conversations.
👥
How does Zapify help me grow?
Zapify turns comments into conversations with automated DMs, lead capture, and follower automation to help grow your account.
🤖
Will automated replies sound robotic?
No. Customize replies using your own tone, language, and brand voice so responses feel natural and aligned with how you communicate.
⚙️
Can I customize the automation rules?
Fully. Control triggers, replies, timing, workflows, AI behavior, and audience actions. Set it once and let it run.
📊
Can I track how my automations are performing?
Yes. Monitor conversations, engagement, leads captured, and automation performance from one simple dashboard.
🟨
Is Zapify free? How much does it cost?
You can start completely free, no credit card required. Upgrade anytime as your automation needs grow and cancel whenever you want.
