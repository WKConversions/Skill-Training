# K.B · the desk (a second film on the same script and voice-over)

Karl, 3 October 2026: "same script and voice-over, but a completely different approach in visuals".

**Thesis.** K.B sits at your desk. One top-down desk is the whole film: the business's own work on it (papers, a
notebook, sticky notes, a laptop, a phone) and K.B's badge placed among them, the way K.B works inside the team.
K.B turns the paper work into a system on the screen, the plan in the notebook into the built thing, the
sticky-note ideas into an app, cables the devices together, lets the repetitive form run by itself, grows the
desk, is there every week on the calendar and turns each new note into a feature. Then the desk clears and the
badge is what stays.

**Excluded from the first K.B film:** a headline per phrase, interface cards and chips floating on the page, the
lane of work items, the dot as connector, the Diagnose · Build · Run loop, the version dial, screens on tilting 3D
planes, tool logos orbiting, typed channel messages.

**Kept from the approved film (Karl's lessons):** bright, never a static dark background; the camera only breathes;
no overshoot or bounce (a clean film); the sign-off is the badge and the words only; "K.B", never "K.B Consultancy";
the real badge, never redrawn; the site's hero video not used.

**Allowed patterns:** order numbers, the board's counts, the sticky-note words, the app screens, the form and the
chat are illustrative and claim nothing; the sticky notes use K.B's own service words (CRM, invoices,
follow-ups, reports, support); "software", "automation" and "AI agent" are K.B's two pillars.

**Few, big things** (Karl: v3 "a bit too busy"): at most four or five objects on the desk at once; the laptop is the
stage in the middle, one object beside it on each side; screen text 36 px or more where it must be read.

**Sound:** the desk's own sounds, picked by ear for a calm, premium, human vibe: paper slides and soft swishes under
the whips (the swish peaking on the cut), soft taps for things set down, a soft pop per button, a chime for the AI
answer, ticks for the calendar marks; the music bed of the first film under it all, the moves that carry no meaning
left to the music.
