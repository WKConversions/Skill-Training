# Nuvolos · landing-page hero film

30 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, silent and seamlessly looping (the network drifts on a 450-frame cycle; frame 899
matches frame 0). Built in Remotion in Nuvolos' own look from nuvolos.com: the deep-blue hero with glowing
cyan network nodes and depth blur, white illustration cards with #3381FF outlines, dashed infrastructure
boxes, the "Nuvolos Reproducibility & Sovereignty Layer" slab, the snapshot timeline, Overpass type.

Story: research scattered (code on a laptop, data in a bucket, apps installed by hand, results in an email)
→ one Computational Workspace with the familiar tools → it runs on any infrastructure through the Nuvolos
layer → snapshots make every result reproducible; collaborate globally, retain control → 65+ universities,
national banks and research institutions → logo and "The Unified Workspace for Sovereign AI & Science."

`npx remotion studio` to edit; `node scripts/stills.mjs 60,240,400` for test frames.
