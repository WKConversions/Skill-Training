Direction

Purpose

Introduce WKConversions on the website, LinkedIn and in outreach: who they are, what they do, and why it works.

Audience

Founders and marketers at SaaS, e-commerce and brands whose product is hard to explain.

Core message

Your visitors give you three seconds; two motion designers turn your paragraph into a video people get in that time.

Style and brand

No intake style chosen, so the WKConversions look: light page with the dot grid, ink type, blue for what matters, Inter Tight 800 headlines.

Art direction

One built world, no stock: the visitor's browser, real founder photos, the real ClearScaler work. No mixed media beyond that.

Motion personality

Precise and settled: arrivals on cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), no bounce, a camera that never locks, motion blur on fast moves.

Transition family

Camera reveal (zoom out of an object), object becomes object (paragraph → video), scale-through into the next frame.

Voice-over and music

Not recorded. Script timed at about 2.5 words per second; music to be chosen.

Open questions

  1. Script. No script was given, so this one is mine, built from the site's own copy. Change any line and the build follows.
  2. Voice-over. Will you or Raphael record it? Send the file and I'll re-time every hit to the real words.
  3. Music. A licensed track, or does music go on in the edit? The cut would follow its beat.
  4. ClearScaler. OK to show their clip and Magnus's words? I used only the UI animation part of the case video, not the film footage.
  5. Formats. 16:9 only, or also a 9:16 cut for social (a re-layout, not a crop)?

Notes and assumptions

  • You asked for the video directly, so I built and rendered it before this approval step instead of after. Revisions go through the same project.
  • Format 16:9 at 30 s, picked for a "who we are" film on the site and LinkedIn.
  • Illustrative, not claims: "yourproduct.com", the finance paragraph and the countdown reading "2.1s" stand for a visitor's experience. They aren't a client or a statistic.
  • Every factual line comes from wkconversions.com: "Two motion designers" (About), Raphael and Karl, the ClearScaler case and Magnus's testimonial ("…the work came back fast and better than the brief"), "Motion design that makes it click."
  • Kept clear of the September ad: no highlight hopping through a paragraph, no keyframe timeline, no 16:9/1:1/9:16 split, no founders' chat.
