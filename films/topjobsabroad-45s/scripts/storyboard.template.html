Notes

  • The film is silent. Every beat is timed to your script at about 2.7 words per second, so a voice-over can be recorded to picture and laid on without re-cutting.
  • Everything on screen comes from topjobsabroad.com: the open roles (Benalmádena, Sliema, Madrid), the six steps, the FAQ answer, the review by Øyvind Kato Olsen and “free for candidates”. Nothing is invented.
  • The script line “Top Jobs Abroad made recruitment comfortable” reads as past tense. On screen it is “Recruitment, made comfortable.” For the voice-over, “makes recruitment comfortable” may sound more natural.
