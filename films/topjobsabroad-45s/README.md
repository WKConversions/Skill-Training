# Top Jobs Abroad · 45 s explainer

45 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Hook → problem → solution → showcase → outro, laid out to the client's script and
timed at about 2.7 words per second, so a voice-over can be recorded straight onto it. Built in Remotion in Top Jobs
Abroad's own look from topjobsabroad.com: deep navy #001135 sections and the light #F4F6FB page, the gold
gradient on their buttons, gold eyebrows, Playfair Display 600 headlines with the key words in gold, Plus Jakarta
Sans for UI, city photography under a navy gradient, white job cards with a photo header, flags and pills.

| s | beat | on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | hook | Madrid at sunset, "This could be your next top move." |
| 3–15 | problem | city cards scatter with "?" badges: "Finding a job abroad can be difficult." Where to start / look / who to contact / how it works |
| 15–18 | solution | the cards fall into a grid: "That's where [Top Jobs Abroad] comes in." |
| 18–27 | showcase | How we help: their real open roles, one matched to you, interview briefing, a question from their FAQ, a progress tracker |
| 27–35 | showcase | "From the start to the end.": their 6 steps, then documents & relocation, "Welcome to Lisbon." |
| 35–38 | showcase | "Recruitment, made comfortable." with a verified review from their site |
| 38–45 | outro | "And the best part? It's completely free." then the logo, "I'm looking for a job", topjobsabroad.com |

Every fact on screen comes from their site: the roles, the steps, the FAQ answer, the review, and "free for
candidates". City photos are Unsplash (the same kind their site uses). `npx remotion studio` to edit;
`node scripts/stills.mjs 60,480,740` for test frames; `python3 scripts/motion_check.py out/draft.mp4`.
