About us
At K.B, we grow with you.
Our work starts with understanding how your business actually runs: the people, the processes, and the potential for improvement. From there, we design tailored workflows, strategies, and tools that simplify operations and create lasting value.
We believe in practical transformation that combines strategy, structure, and technology to help teams work better, faster, and with more clarity. Every project we take on is guided by a long-term mindset, built to scale, adapt, and evolve alongside your business.
When you partner with us, you gain more than a service provider; you gain a strategic partner invested in your growth. We treat your goals like our own and continuously learn, test, and improve, both internally and through close collaboration with our clients and partners.
Our Mission
To be the technical team you don't have to hire in-house.
Our Vision
To be the tech partner European SMEs and startups turn to before they build their own tech team.
Our DNA
Technical

From generalist consultancy to embedded tech partner
K.B started out offering a bit of everything: consulting, automation, software, whatever a client needed. Over time we noticed the businesses that got the most out of working with us weren't looking for advice. They wanted someone to build the thing and stay involved.
So we narrowed our focus. Today we build software and automation, and we build it together with our clients, then stay on to run it. That's the whole model now, built in from day one.
