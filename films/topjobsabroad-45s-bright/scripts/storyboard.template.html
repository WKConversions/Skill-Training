What changed from the first version

  • Bright. The light page, white cards and daylight photos from their site instead of navy sections.
  • Smoother. The first version reset its camera push at every beat (a small jump each time) and faded layouts in place. Now each scene has one continuous camera, and every change is a move: a pull-back, tiles falling, a collapse into a point, a page pan, a circle opening from a pin, a whip pan, a card flip.
  • More variety. A photo wall, a map with routes, a search window, chat, paperwork, a conveyor of job cards, a video call, a signed contract, a plane on a route, stamps, a receipt, a click. No two beats use the same device.
  • More visual. Real faces and places carry the film: a traveller, a candidate, a recruiter, a new home, their destinations. One real role (Danish Speaking Customer Support, Athens) runs through the showcase.
  • Measured. 99% of frames moving, a median 5.7% of the frame in motion (the first version: 2.0%; Karl's benchmark: 3.2%), longest still 0.17 s.
  • Same script timing as the first version, about 2.7 words per second, so one voice-over fits both.
