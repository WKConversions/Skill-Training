Founder · Málaga, Spain
More than 12 years at the intersection of technology, go-to-market and strategic partnerships.
Amargier Advisory brings senior ISV and partnership experience from Oracle and EDB to technology, software and platform businesses.
That experience includes working across a territory of more than 300 ISVs at Oracle, and later leading seven EMEA business development managers focused on cloud adoption and go-to-market with ISVs.
Today, the focus is helping businesses build partnership models that are clear, repeatable and commercially useful as they grow.
