# Sound and Motion

## Relationship

Sound should reinforce motion hierarchy.

A sound is most useful when it:
- confirms an impact,
- clarifies a transition,
- adds scale,
- creates anticipation,
- reinforces texture,
- establishes environment,
- guides rhythm.

## Layers

Possible layers:
- music,
- ambience/bed,
- impacts,
- whooshes,
- UI interactions,
- risers,
- tonal transitions,
- textures.

Do not add a whoosh to every movement.

## Sync

Perfect one-to-one sync for every animation can feel mechanical.

Use exact sync for key impacts.
Use near-sync and layered timing for secondary movement.

## Silence

Reduced sound can make a following impact stronger.

Treat silence or low-density sound as part of the rhythm.
