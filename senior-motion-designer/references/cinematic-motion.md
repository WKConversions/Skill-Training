# Cinematic Motion

## Cinematic does not mean slow

Cinematic quality comes from:
- intentional framing,
- motivated camera movement,
- depth,
- lighting,
- pacing,
- anticipation,
- sound,
- controlled information reveal.

## Camera movement

A camera move needs motivation.

Use it to:
- reveal information,
- follow action,
- transfer attention,
- create scale,
- connect scenes,
- intensify a moment.

Avoid constant drifting camera motion.

## Lens and depth feeling

Even in 2.5D:
- foreground objects should move more than background objects during parallax,
- focus can transfer attention,
- perspective should remain coherent,
- depth should support hierarchy.

## Cuts

Not every transition needs interpolation.

A clean hard cut can be stronger than a forced transition when:
- audio provides the connection,
- composition matches,
- action direction matches,
- contrast is desired.

## Atmosphere

Particles, grain, glow, bloom, haze, and lens effects must support the art direction.

Do not use atmosphere to hide weak composition.
