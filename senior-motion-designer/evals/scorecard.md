# Motion Design Evaluation Scorecard

Use this to compare iterations consistently.

Score each dimension 1–5, then explain evidence. Do not optimize the numeric total at the expense of judgment.

## Communication
5 = visual meaning is immediate and reinforces the message.
1 = animation is attractive but unclear or unrelated.

## Composition
5 = strong hierarchy, spacing, balance, crop, and focal control.
1 = weak or template-like arrangement.

## Typography
5 = type is designed, readable, hierarchical, and integrated.
1 = subtitle-like or inconsistent.

## Motion
5 = controlled, purposeful, well-choreographed movement.
1 = arbitrary movement or default easing.

## Timing and rhythm
5 = varied, deliberate, readable pacing.
1 = monotonous, rushed, or dragging.

## Transitions and continuity
5 = scenes connect through a coherent visual grammar.
1 = transitions are unrelated effects.

## Art direction
5 = distinct and consistent visual world.
1 = mixed visual styles or generic AI aesthetic.

## Product/brand accuracy
5 = accurately represents supplied brand/product.
1 = invents or distorts important information.

## Sound relationship
5 = audio reinforces hierarchy and rhythm.
1 = random or excessive SFX.

## Polish
5 = professional commercial finish.
1 = visible roughness, broken spacing, awkward curves, unreadable frames.

## Review questions

- Which scene is weakest?
- Which scene is strongest and why?
- What looks generic?
- What looks over-designed?
- What can be removed?
- Where does the eye go incorrectly?
- Which transition breaks continuity?
- Which timing decision feels least intentional?
- What three changes produce the largest quality gain?
