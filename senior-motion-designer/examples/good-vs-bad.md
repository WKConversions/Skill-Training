# Good vs Bad Motion Decisions

## Script: "Everything you need, in one place."

Weak:
Five unrelated icons fly in from different sides with bounce.

Stronger:
Several fragmented panels occupy separate regions. A single spatial action draws them toward one shared
container. The container settles; the headline appears only after the consolidation is visually clear.

Why:
The motion itself communicates consolidation.

---

## Script: "Move faster."

Weak:
Text says MOVE FASTER and shakes rapidly.

Stronger:
Establish several slow sequential steps. Then collapse the distance between them into one continuous
movement. Show the same outcome occurring in a fraction of the space/time.

Why:
The viewer experiences the reduction in friction.

---

## Script: "See what matters."

Weak:
Eye icon + sparkle.

Stronger:
Begin with dense low-contrast information. Depth/focus and contrast isolate the one meaningful signal while
the rest recedes.

Why:
Hierarchy performs the meaning.

---

## UI feature

Weak:
Full dashboard screenshot scales from 90% to 100%, cursor clicks one button.

Stronger:
Establish enough dashboard for context. Shift focus to the relevant card. Other UI subtly recedes.
The chosen card becomes the spatial bridge into a detailed feature view.

Why:
The animation controls attention instead of asking the viewer to search the interface.
